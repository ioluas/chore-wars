
# <img src="PalestinianTux.png" width="128" height="128" valign="middle" alt="Eddar"> Eddar

Eddar is an Android application designed to simplify household and team chore management. It automates the cycling 
and rotation of tasks among members to ensure a fair and organized living or working environment.

## Milestones

- [ ] Team & Household Management: Create, join, and manage multiple households or teams.
- [ ] Chore Definition: Detailed chore configuration including frequency (configurable), 
      description, and difficulty scoring.
- [ ] Smart Rotation: Automated assignment and periodic cycling of chores among active members.
- [ ] Push Notifications: Timely reminders for new assignments and overdue tasks.
